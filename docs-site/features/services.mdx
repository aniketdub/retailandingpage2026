The service catalogue is the smallest module and the one everything else depends on. The calendar, the AI agent, billing, inventory, and packages all read from it.

## Fields on a service

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What appears on the invoice and what Aisha calls it to patients |
| **Category** | Groups the catalogue — Laser, Injectables, Facials, Consultation, Trichology |
| **Price** | Base price before any package or discount |
| **GST rate** | Per service, not per clinic. Applied per line at billing. |
| **Duration** | Sets the slot length on the calendar and constrains what the AI can offer |
| **Clinicians** | Who may deliver it. The calendar and the AI both respect this. |
| **Consumables** | Bill of materials deducted from stock on delivery |
| **Pre/post care** | Instructions sent automatically around the appointment |
| **Patient-facing description** | What Aisha tells a patient who asks what this treatment involves |

<Warning>
  Duration and clinician mapping are load-bearing. A wrong duration produces double-booked chairs; a missing clinician mapping means the AI cannot offer that service at all, and patients asking for it get escalated instead of booked.
</Warning>

## The patient-facing description

This field is what Aisha reads when a patient asks "what is a HydraFacial?" or "how long does laser take?". Write it as you would say it to a patient.

<Tip>
  Include what the session involves, how long it takes, roughly what to expect afterwards, and the price. Leave out anything you would not want stated without a consultation — outcome promises, in particular. Aisha will not invent claims, but she will faithfully repeat one you wrote.
</Tip>

## Categories and ordering

Categories control grouping on the checkout screen and in reports. Order services within a category so your highest-volume items are first — the front desk is picking from this list forty times a day.

## Price changes

Editing a price affects future invoices only. Existing invoices and sold packages keep the price they were sold at; historical revenue never restates.

<Note>
  Sold packages hold their own agreed price. Raising the price of a component service does not change what an existing package holder owes.
</Note>

## Bulk import

Long price lists come in by CSV — name, category, price, GST rate, duration. See [Data import](/admin/data-import).
