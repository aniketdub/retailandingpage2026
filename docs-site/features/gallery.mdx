Before-and-after photographs are the strongest sales asset an aesthetic clinic owns and the most sensitive data it holds. RetAI treats them as both.

## Capturing photos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the patient or the session" icon="user">
    Photos can be captured from the patient profile, or directly from a package session so the linkage is automatic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture or upload" icon="camera">
    Use the device camera — the mobile app makes this a one-tap job at the chair — or upload from a DSLR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag it" icon="tags">
    Treatment, body area, angle (front, three-quarter, left, right), and whether it is a *before*, *progress*, or *after* image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record consent" icon="file-signature">
    Two separate permissions, captured separately:

    - **Clinical record** — always required. The photo is part of the medical record.
    - **Marketing use** — optional and explicit. Without it, the photo is structurally incapable of entering a shareable collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Marketing consent is not a display setting that someone can toggle off later. A photo without recorded marketing consent cannot be added to a shareable collection at all — the export path refuses it. This is deliberate: under India's DPDP Act, "we forgot to blur it" is not a defence.
</Warning>

## Comparison view

Select any two images of the same patient and area to open a side-by-side or slider comparison, with the date and session number on each. Because photos bind to the package session they were taken at, before/after pairs assemble themselves rather than needing manual curation.

## Editing tools

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Crop, rotate, and align" icon="crop">
    Align a follow-up photo to the framing of the original so the comparison is honest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clinic watermark" icon="droplet">
    Applies your logo at a configured position and opacity. Set it once in [Clinic setup](/admin/clinic-setup) and it applies to every exported image.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Identity masking" icon="eye-slash">
    Blur or bar the eyes and any identifying marks. On any export where marketing consent covers the treatment area but not facial identity, masking is required before the export completes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Annotation" icon="pen">
    Mark treatment sites or areas of interest directly on the image. Annotations are stored as a layer, so the original is never altered.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Search and filters

Filter by treatment, body area, clinician, date range, consent state, and whether a matching after-photo exists. The last one is the useful one: **"before photos with no after photo"** is a list of patients who did not finish their course.

## Collections

Group images into a collection — "Laser hair reduction results", "Acne scar revision" — for use in counselling. Collections can be shown to a prospective patient on a tablet during consultation, or exported.

<Note>
  Every image in a collection must carry marketing consent. Attempting to add one that does not is refused with the reason stated, not silently skipped.
</Note>

## Storage and access

Photographs are stored encrypted, scoped to your clinic, and served only through short-lived signed URLs to signed-in staff. A URL copied from the browser expires and becomes useless — it cannot be forwarded as a permanent link.
