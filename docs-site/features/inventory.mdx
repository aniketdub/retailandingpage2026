Aesthetic clinics lose real money to stock they cannot account for — a vial short at the end of the month with no record of where it went. RetAI ties consumption to the procedure that consumed it.

## Products

Every stock item carries:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name and SKU | |
| Category | Consumable, injectable, retail, pharmacy |
| Unit | Vial, ml, piece, box — the unit you actually count in |
| Purchase and selling price | Selling price only applies to retail and pharmacy items |
| GST rate | Applied per line when sold |
| Reorder level | Triggers a low-stock alert |
| Expiry tracking | On for anything with a shelf life |
| Batch tracking | On for injectables and pharmacy items |

## Bill of materials

Attach consumables to a service and the quantity each session uses. When a session is delivered, stock is deducted automatically — no separate stock entry, and no month-end guesswork about where the vials went.

<Frame caption="A procedure's bill of materials.">
  <img src="/images/features/inventory-bom.png" alt="Procedure bill of materials" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Set the bill of materials once, at onboarding, for your ten highest-volume procedures. That covers most of your consumption. Add the long tail later.
</Tip>

## Stock movements

Every change in quantity is a ledger entry with a reason and a person attached:

- **Purchase received** — against a vendor and purchase order
- **Consumed by procedure** — automatic, linked to the appointment
- **Dispensed to patient** — pharmacy or retail sale, linked to the invoice
- **Internal issue** — moved to another room or location
- **Adjustment** — physical count correction, reason required
- **Wastage** — breakage, contamination, expiry, reason required
- **Return to vendor**

<Note>
  There is no way to change a quantity without leaving a ledger row. That is the entire point of the module.
</Note>

## Vendors and purchase orders

Maintain vendor records with contact details and payment terms. Raise a purchase order, send it, receive against it — partially if that is what arrived — and stock updates on receipt. Outstanding POs and short deliveries are visible on the inventory overview.

## Expiry and batches

Batch-tracked items record batch number and expiry on receipt. Consumption is FIFO by expiry date, so the oldest stock goes first. Items approaching expiry surface on the overview at a threshold you set.

<Warning>
  Expiry alerts depend on batch data being entered at receipt. Skipping it at the goods-in stage means no expiry warning later — the system cannot warn about a date nobody recorded.
</Warning>

## Prescription fulfilment

A prescription written in [Consultations & Rx](/features/consultations) can be dispensed here: stock deducts, the items are added to the patient's invoice, and the dispensing is recorded against the prescription.

## Stock take

Enter counted quantities against system quantities. RetAI shows the variance per item and its value, and posts the adjustments as ledger entries with your stock-take reference.

## Reports

Consumption by procedure, consumption by clinician, stock value on hand, wastage rate, expiry write-offs, and reorder suggestions based on run rate.

<Tip>
  **Consumption by clinician** is the report nobody asks for and everybody should read. Two clinicians doing the same procedure with materially different consumption is either a training issue or a leak.
</Tip>
