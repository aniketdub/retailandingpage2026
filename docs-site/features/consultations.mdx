Free-text notes are unsearchable and unauditable. RetAI consultations are structured forms, built per specialty, that still let a clinician draw exactly what they saw.

<Note>
  Prescribing is off for the receptionist role by default. An owner can grant it in [Roles & permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>

## Consultation templates

Each template is a set of fields your clinic defines: presenting complaint, history, examination findings, diagnosis, plan, and any specialty sections you need — Fitzpatrick type, hair-loss grade, acne severity, prior treatments, contraindications.

## The clinical drawing tool

Some findings are faster drawn than described. The annotation canvas gives the clinician a clean anatomical base diagram to mark on.

<Frame caption="Marking treatment sites on the face — front diagram.">
  <img src="/images/features/annotation-canvas.png" alt="Clinical annotation canvas" />
</Frame>

**Base diagrams available:**

| Diagram | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Face — front | Injectables, peels, lesion mapping |
| Face — three-quarter | Profile-dependent work, jawline, temple |
| Scalp — vertex | Hair-loss grading, graft planning, PRP sites |
| Body — front | Laser hair reduction areas, body contouring |

The diagrams are deliberately minimal line art — every line the diagram contributes is a line competing with the clinician's own marks. Landmarks appear only where they are needed to place an annotation accurately.

**Tools:** freehand pen, straight line, shaded region, point marker with a numbered label, text callout, and colour selection for distinguishing planned versus completed sites.

<Tip>
  All diagrams share one coordinate space. Switching the base diagram — face front to three-quarter, say — does not invalidate marks already recorded. Annotations from successive visits can be overlaid to show progression.
</Tip>

## Prescriptions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a template" icon="file-lines">
    Your common regimens — acne protocol, post-laser aftercare, melasma maintenance — saved with drugs, strengths, frequency, duration, and instructions already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust for the patient" icon="pen-to-square">
    Every field is editable. Allergies and clinical flags from the patient record are shown while you prescribe, not buried a tab away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and issue" icon="signature">
    The prescription carries your clinic letterhead, the prescriber's name and registration number, and the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver" icon="paper-plane">
    Print, download as PDF, or send to the patient over WhatsApp. Delivery is logged on the patient record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pharmacy fulfilment

If you run an in-house pharmacy, a prescription can be dispensed against [Inventory](/features/inventory) — stock is deducted and the items are added to the invoice.

## Aftercare instructions

Attach pre- and post-procedure instructions to any service. They send automatically at the right time relative to the appointment: pre-care the day before, aftercare an hour after the visit completes.

<Warning>
  Consultation notes and prescriptions are clinical records. They are versioned and audited — edits are recorded with who and when, and nothing is silently overwritten. See [Retention & deletion](/security/retention-and-deletion) for how long they are kept.
</Warning>
