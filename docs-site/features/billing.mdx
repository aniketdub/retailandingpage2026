## Checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the patient" icon="user">
    Or start from an appointment, which pre-fills the patient and the service delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items" icon="plus">
    Services, packages, retail products, or a free-text item for anything not in the catalogue. Quantities and per-line discounts are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the total" icon="calculator">
    GST is calculated **per line at that line's own rate** and shown live. A bill mixing 5% and 18% items is never blended into a single wrong average rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take payment" icon="credit-card">
    One method or several — see split payments below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue" icon="receipt">
    The invoice number is allocated atomically from a per-clinic sequence. Two tills billing at the same instant cannot produce the same invoice number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment methods

Cash, card, UPI, bank transfer, cheque, package redemption, and clinic credit.

### Split payments

One invoice, several tenders — ₹5,000 on UPI and ₹3,000 in cash, say. Each part is recorded separately so your day-end reconciliation by method actually balances.

### Partial payment and outstanding

Take less than the full amount and the remainder is recorded as outstanding against the patient. Outstanding balances appear on the patient profile, in the day-end report, and in the overdue-accounts list.

## Retainer and credit

Patients who pay in advance against future treatment hold a **retainer balance**. Refunds, goodwill adjustments, and overpayments create **clinic credit**. Both are redeemable at checkout and both appear on the patient profile, so the front desk cannot miss that a patient already has money on account.

## Invoice list and search

Filter by date range, patient, payment method, staff member, service, or outstanding status. Export any filtered view to CSV for your accountant — see [Data export](/admin/data-export).

## Day-end close

The day-end report reconciles: total collected by method, invoices issued, discounts granted with who granted them, refunds, outstanding created, and GST collected by rate.

<Tip>
  Run day-end before the last person leaves, not the next morning. A discrepancy is trivially traceable while the person who took the payment is still in the building.
</Tip>

See [Month-end close](/workflows/month-end-close) for the monthly version.

## Amendments, refunds, and cancellation

Issued invoices are not silently editable — they are tax documents.

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Amend** | Creates a revision. Both versions are kept and linked. |
| **Refund** | Recorded against the original invoice with a reason. |
| **Cancel** | The invoice is voided, not deleted. It stays in the sequence and in reports. |

<Warning>
  Refunds and cancellations require billing permission and are audited with the staff member's name. This is a control, not an inconvenience.
</Warning>

## A known limitation, stated plainly

Invoice creation writes the invoice, its line items, and the ledger entry as a sequence rather than as a single atomic transaction. In the rare event of an infrastructure failure mid-write, a partially built invoice can result. RetAI logs these and they are reconcilable, but we would rather you knew than discovered it.
