## Revenue

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Revenue summary" icon="chart-line" defaultOpen>
    Collected versus billed, by day, week, or month. Split by payment method, service category, and clinician. Package revenue appears as it is earned, not as it is collected — see [Packages](/features/packages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="GST summary" icon="percent">
    Tax collected by rate, with taxable value per rate. The report your accountant asks for.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Outstanding and ageing" icon="clock">
    Who owes what, bucketed by age. Sorted by amount so the calls worth making are at the top.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discounts granted" icon="tag">
    Every discount with the staff member who granted it. Discount as a percentage of gross is the number to watch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Retainer and credit" icon="wallet">
    Advance money held and credit outstanding. Both are liabilities, and both are easy to forget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Retention — the reports that justify the product

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Revenue recovered" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    Bookings that came from an at-risk outreach, attributed to the message that produced them. The honest measure of RetAI's return.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow-up performance" icon="paper-plane">
    Messages sent, replied to, and converted to a booking — by message type and by timing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Retention rate" icon="repeat">
    Share of patients returning within their expected interval, trended over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Package completion" icon="box">

## Clinical and operational

| Report | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| Appointments overview | Booked, completed, cancelled, no-show — by day, clinician, and service |
| No-show analysis | Who, when, which slots, and whether reminders were delivered |
| Procedure trends | Which treatments are growing and which are fading, period over period |
| Clinician utilisation | Booked hours against available hours |
| Chair and room utilisation | Where the physical bottleneck actually is |
| Patient demographics | Age, gender, location, referral source |
| New versus returning | New patient acquisition against returning volume |
| Feedback analytics | Post-visit ratings and comments, by clinician and service |
| Incentive payouts | Staff commission based on your configured rules |
| Consumption by procedure | From [Inventory](/features/inventory) |

## Day-end report

The close-of-business reconciliation: collections by method, invoices issued, discounts, refunds, outstanding created, GST collected by rate, and appointments completed against booked.

<Tip>
  Run it before the last person leaves. A ₹500 discrepancy is trivially traceable while the person who took the payment is still there, and near-impossible three days later.
</Tip>

## Scheduled reports

Any report can be scheduled to arrive by email or WhatsApp on a cadence — day-end nightly to the owner, revenue summary every Monday, package expiry every Friday.

## Exports

Every report exports to CSV. A full clinic export — patients, appointments, invoices, and treatments in one archive — is available to owners from [Data export](/admin/data-export).
