<Note>
  The Staff module is visible to **owners only** by default, and cannot be granted to a receptionist role. A clinic should not be able to lock out its own administrator.
</Note>

## Adding a staff member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Staff → Add staff member" icon="user-plus">
    Name, work email, phone, and role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role" icon="user-shield">
    Owner, Doctor, or Receptionist. The role sets the default module access; you can override it per module afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set clinical details, if applicable" icon="stethoscope">
    For doctors: registration number, specialty, signature for prescriptions, and which services they may deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite" icon="envelope">
    They receive sign-in instructions. Google sign-in works immediately for the email you entered; there is no self-registration path, so an unrecognised Google account cannot create itself access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles

| Role | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Every module, always. Cannot be restricted. Can manage staff, permissions, and clinic settings. |
| **Doctor** | Clinical and commercial modules. No staff management by default. |
| **Receptionist** | Front-of-house modules. No billing, inventory, or prescriptions by default. |

Full defaults are in the [module tour](/features/overview#default-access-by-role).

## The permission matrix

Toggle any module on or off for the Doctor and Receptionist roles, per clinic. Changes take effect on the staff member's next page load.

<Warning>
  Access is enforced on the server when a page loads, not hidden in the browser. Typing the URL of a module you lack access to returns nothing. Do not rely on "they don't know the URL" — but equally, do not worry that they might.
</Warning>

## Clinician availability

Set working days, hours, breaks, and leave per clinician. This constrains the calendar and constrains what the AI agent can offer — a slot outside a clinician's hours will not be booked by Aisha.

## Presence

The staff list shows who is currently signed in and active. Useful at a busy front desk for knowing who can pick up an escalated WhatsApp thread right now.

## De-provisioning

Deactivate a staff member and their access ends. Two properties are worth knowing:

- **Their session does not survive.** Role and clinic are re-read from the staff record periodically, so a removed staff member loses access within minutes — they cannot ride out the remaining lifetime of an already-issued session.
- **Their records survive.** Consultations, invoices, and messages they created stay attached to their name. Clinical and financial history does not disappear because someone left.

<Tip>
  Deactivate, do not delete. Deletion of a staff member who has ever signed a prescription would orphan a clinical record.
</Tip>

## Audit

Sign-ins, permission changes, discount grants, refunds, record edits, and data exports are logged with the staff member and timestamp. See [Access control](/security/access-control).
