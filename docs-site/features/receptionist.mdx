Every missed call at an aesthetic clinic is a patient who called a competitor next. The AI Receptionist answers on the lines your team cannot get to — after hours, during procedures, or when both handsets are already busy.

## What a call looks like

<Steps>
  <Step title="The call is answered" icon="phone">
    Either every call, or only calls that ring unanswered past a threshold. Your choice — see [Voice setup](/admin/integrations/voice).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent identifies the caller" icon="id-card">
    If the number matches an existing patient, the agent knows their history. If not, it treats them as a new enquiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It handles the enquiry" icon="comments">
    Clinic hours and location, what a treatment involves and costs, availability, and appointment booking or rescheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It escalates when it should" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Anything clinical, any complaint, or any request it cannot complete is handed off — transferred live if a human is available, otherwise captured as a callback with a summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Everything is logged" icon="file-lines">
    Recording, transcript, outcome, and any created appointment are written to the patient's record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The call log

Each row shows caller, duration, outcome, and attributed patient. Open a call for:

- **Transcript** — full text, searchable
- **Recording** — audio playback, subject to your retention setting
- **Outcome** — booked, enquiry captured, escalated, or no action
- **Linked records** — appointments and leads created by the call

<Note>
  A call's clinic and patient are resolved from the phone number on the server, never from the call payload. A spoofed or malformed payload cannot attribute a call to the wrong clinic.
</Note>

## Outbound calls

Trigger a call from a patient record — confirmation for tomorrow's slot, a win-back for a lapsed high-value patient, or a package-expiry reminder. Outbound calling is rate-limited per clinic.

<Warning>
  Recording consent is your clinic's legal obligation. RetAI plays a configurable consent announcement at call start; you are responsible for the wording matching what your jurisdiction requires. See [Data protection](/security/data-protection).
</Warning>

## Configuring the agent

In [Voice setup](/admin/integrations/voice) you control:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting | The first line the caller hears |
| Voice and language | Including Indian English and regional language options |
| Answer policy | All calls, after-hours only, or overflow only |
| Escalation number | Where live transfers go |
| Booking permission | Whether the agent may create appointments or only capture enquiries |
| Recording retention | How long audio is kept before automatic deletion |

<Tip>
  Start with **overflow only** and **enquiry capture, no booking**. Read a week of transcripts, tune the greeting and the treatment descriptions, then let it book. Clinics that turn everything on at once end up distrusting the transcripts they never read.
</Tip>

## When voice is not enabled

Voice is a separate tier. If your plan does not include it, the module is hidden and no calls are answered — the rest of RetAI is unaffected.
