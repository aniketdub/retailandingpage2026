An aesthetic clinic's real conversion problem is not the consultation — it is the fortnight after it, when a quoted patient goes quiet. The Leads module exists for that fortnight.

## Where leads come from

| Source | How it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp enquiry | An unknown number messages; Aisha handles it and creates the lead |
| Missed or answered call | The [AI Receptionist](/features/receptionist) captures the enquiry |
| Walk-in | Reception creates it by hand |
| Website form | Via the API — see [CRM endpoints](/api-reference/endpoints/crm) |
| Campaign import | CSV upload |

## Pipeline stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="New" icon="inbox">
    Just arrived, no one has spoken to them yet. Unassigned leads are flagged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contacted" icon="phone">
    Someone has spoken to them. Log what was discussed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consultation booked" icon="calendar-check">
    An appointment exists. The lead is linked to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quoted" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    A quotation has been issued. This is where deals sit longest and die quietest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Converted" icon="circle-check">
    Treatment purchased. The lead becomes a patient record; nothing is retyped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lost" icon="circle-xmark">
    With a reason — price, timing, went elsewhere, no response. Lost reasons are the most useful report in this module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Counselling and quotations

The counselling tool is for the conversation where a patient asks "so what would all of this cost?"

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build an editable quotation" icon="pen-to-square" defaultOpen>
    Add services and packages, apply a discount, and RetAI computes GST per line at the correct rate for each item. Save it against the lead. Revise it as many times as the conversation needs — every version is kept.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send it" icon="paper-plane">
    Deliver as a branded PDF over WhatsApp or email. Delivery and open are logged against the lead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Record observations" icon="clipboard-list">
    Free-text counselling notes — concerns raised, budget signalled, objections. These are visible to whoever picks the lead up next, which is the entire point.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Convert to an invoice" icon="receipt">
    When the patient accepts, convert the quotation straight to a checkout. Nothing is re-entered, so the quoted price and the billed price cannot drift apart.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ownership and follow-up

Every lead has an owner and a next-action date. Overdue actions surface on the owner's overview screen and, if enabled, as a push notification.

<Tip>
  Set the next-action date during the counselling conversation, while the patient is still in front of you. A lead with no next action is a lost lead with a delay.
</Tip>

## Reporting

The Leads report shows conversion by source, by owner, and by service; average days from enquiry to conversion; and the breakdown of lost reasons. See [Reports](/features/reports).
