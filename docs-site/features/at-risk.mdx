This is the module the product is named after. Every clinic has patients quietly slipping away — a package half-finished, a maintenance cycle missed, a course of treatment abandoned after session two. They rarely announce it. They just stop coming.

## How the score works

At-risk is **a ranking, not a label.** It weighs:

| Signal | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Days since last visit, relative to *that patient's* own rhythm | A quarterly patient at 11 weeks is fine. A monthly patient at 11 weeks is not. |
| Sessions remaining on a paid package | Already-paid, unredeemed value is the easiest revenue in the clinic to recover. |
| Treatment type and expected cadence | Laser hair reduction has a biological interval. A missed one costs results, not just revenue. |
| Lifetime value | Two patients equally lapsed are not equally worth a phone call. |
| Recent no-shows or cancellations | A cancellation with no rebooking is a stronger drift signal than a long gap alone. |
| Open conversation state | A patient mid-conversation with Aisha is being handled; they do not need a second chase. |

The list is sorted by **revenue at stake**, so the top of the list is the most valuable call your front desk can make today — not merely the longest-absent name.

## Working the list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open At-Risk Patients" icon="heart-pulse">
    The list is recomputed continuously. Work top-down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the reason" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Every row states *why* it is there: "3 of 6 laser sessions remaining, 9 weeks since last visit, package expires in 41 days." You are never guessing at the pitch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft a message" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Click **Draft message**. RetAI writes a message grounded in that patient's real history — the treatment they had, the gap, what is unfinished.

    <Frame caption="An AI-drafted win-back message with its grounding context shown.">
      <img src="/images/features/at-risk-ai-draft.png" alt="AI-drafted win-back message" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and send" icon="paper-plane">
    You always see the draft before it goes. Edit freely. Send over WhatsApp with one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Aisha take the reply" icon="robot">
    When the patient answers, Aisha handles the conversation and books the slot. The row leaves the at-risk list when the appointment is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the AI will and will not say

The drafting model is constrained. It may reference the patient's own treatment history, sessions remaining, and your published service catalogue.

It is forbidden from:

- Promising a clinical outcome or result timeline
- Inventing a discount, offer, or price not in your catalogue
- Claiming an appointment exists that has not been created
- Making any medical claim about the patient's condition

<Note>
  Every AI-drafted message is logged with the context it was given. If a patient ever queries what your clinic told them, the record is complete.
</Note>

## Bulk campaigns

Filter the list — for example *"package expiring within 30 days, sessions remaining"* — and send a personalised draft to everyone matching. Each message is generated per patient from their own history; this is not a mail merge with a name field.

<Warning>
  Bulk sending is rate-limited and spread over time to protect your WhatsApp number's sending reputation. A hundred identical messages in one minute is how a business number gets restricted.
</Warning>

## Measuring recovery

The [Reports](/features/reports) module tracks **revenue recovered** — bookings that came from an at-risk outreach, attributed to the message that produced them. That number is the honest measure of whether RetAI is paying for itself.
