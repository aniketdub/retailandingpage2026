WhatsApp is where Indian clinics actually talk to patients. RetAI treats it as the primary channel, not a notification afterthought.

## The inbox

Threads sort by most recent message. Each thread shows the patient's name (or their number if they are not yet a patient), a preview, and an unread badge. Selecting a thread opens the conversation alongside that patient's context — active packages, last visit, upcoming appointment, outstanding balance.

Filters: **Unread**, **Needs attention**, **Handled by AI**, **Assigned to me**.

## Aisha — what she does unprompted

When a patient messages, Aisha reads the recent conversation, that patient's active treatment plan, and your live service catalogue. Then she either replies or acts.

  <Card title="Answers questions" icon="circle-question">
    What a treatment involves, what it costs, how long a session takes, what aftercare is required — all from your own catalogue, never invented.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Books appointments" icon="calendar-check">
    Finds a genuinely free slot with a qualified clinician for the requested service, and creates the appointment.

    Moves an existing booking and re-queues the reminder.

  <Card title="Escalates" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Anything clinical, any complaint, anything she is not confident about goes to a human and the thread is flagged **Needs attention**.

### What she will never do

<Warning>
  These are enforced in code, not merely requested in a prompt:

  - **She cannot cross patients.** Which clinic and which patient a conversation belongs to is resolved on the server from the verified message sender. A crafted message can steer what Aisha *says*; it cannot make her read or write another patient's record.
  - **She cannot confirm a booking that was not made.** Replies claiming an appointment that no tool actually created are intercepted before they reach the patient.
  - **She cannot make clinical or outcome claims.** A forbidden-claims filter runs on every outgoing message.
  - **She cannot invent prices or offers.** Numbers come from your service catalogue.
</Warning>

## Taking over from the AI

Click **Take over** on any thread. Aisha stops replying to that conversation and it becomes yours. Your replies send under the same clinic number, so the patient sees one continuous conversation.

Click **Hand back** to return the thread to the AI.

<Tip>
  Threads marked **Needs attention** are the ones Aisha deliberately did not answer. Working that filter first is the fastest way to keep the inbox honest.
</Tip>

## Templates and quick replies

For the messages you send constantly — directions to the clinic, pre-procedure instructions, parking guidance — save a quick reply and insert it with two clicks. Templates support patient-name and appointment-time placeholders.

## Automatic messages

Beyond conversation, RetAI sends on its own:

| Message | When |
| --- | --- |
| Appointment reminder | Configurable, typically 24h and 2h before |
| Confirmation request | On tentative bookings |
| Post-visit follow-up | After a completed appointment |
| Aftercare instructions | On procedures configured to send them |
| Package session reminder | When sessions remain and the gap is growing |
| Invoice delivery | On demand from [Billing](/features/billing) |
| Win-back | From [At-Risk Patients](/features/at-risk) |

Timing rules are set in [Clinic setup](/admin/clinic-setup).

## Delivery and duplicates

Every message is logged with its delivery status. Two properties are worth knowing:

- **Nothing sends twice.** Queued messages are claimed atomically before sending, so overlapping workers cannot double-message a patient.
- **Inbound messages are deduplicated at the database level.** WhatsApp retries a webhook on any hiccup; a retried delivery cannot produce a second reply from Aisha.
