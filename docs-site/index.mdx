RetAI is the clinic operating system built by **RetentionOS** for dermatology, skin, hair, and aesthetic practices. It runs the front desk, the clinical record, the till, and the stock room — and it adds something legacy clinic software does not have: **an AI that talks to your patients on WhatsApp and over the phone, and books them back in.**

## What makes RetAI different

Most clinic software records what already happened. RetAI is built around what *should* happen next.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Aisha on WhatsApp" icon="comments" href="/features/chats">
    An AI agent that reads the conversation, answers in your clinic's voice, and books the appointment itself — no template menus.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI voice receptionist" icon="phone-volume" href="/features/receptionist">
    Answers calls your front desk misses, captures the enquiry, and logs it against the patient record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="At-risk scoring" icon="heart-pulse" href="/features/at-risk">
    Ranks patients who are drifting away by how much revenue is walking out with them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
