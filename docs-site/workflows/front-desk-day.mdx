Written for the person at the desk. Follow it top to bottom for the first fortnight and it becomes habit.

## Opening — first 15 minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calendar, day view" icon="calendar-days">
    Confirm every clinician's column looks right. Anything in **Tentative** amber needs a confirmation call or message today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the WhatsApp inbox" icon="comments">
    Filter to **Needs attention** first — those are the threads Aisha deliberately did not answer overnight. Then work **Unread**.

    <Tip>
      Do not read every AI-handled thread each morning. Skim the flagged ones. Reading all of them is how the inbox becomes a job instead of a tool.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check missed calls" icon="phone-volume">
    Open the AI Receptionist call log filtered to overnight. Anything marked *escalated* or *callback requested* needs a human call this morning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review today's arrivals" icon="list-check">
    For each patient arriving today, glance at: outstanding balance, active package sessions remaining, and whether a photo is due this session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Running the floor

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Patient arrives" icon="door-open" defaultOpen>
    Find them on the calendar and mark **Checked in**. If they are new, complete the patient record — phone number with country code, always.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Patient is with the clinician" icon="stethoscope">
    Nothing for you to do. The clinician writes the consultation and any prescription. If a session photograph is due, they or you capture it against the session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Patient comes back to the desk" icon="receipt">
    In one flow:

    1. Mark the appointment **Completed** — this triggers the post-visit follow-up and deducts consumables
    2. Open checkout, add what was delivered
    3. Take payment; split across methods if needed
    4. Send the invoice over WhatsApp
    5. Book the next session before they leave

    <Warning>
      Step 5 is the one that gets skipped when the desk is busy, and it is the one that matters most. A patient who leaves without a next appointment is the patient who ends up on the at-risk list eight weeks later.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Phone rings and you cannot get to it" icon="phone">
    The AI Receptionist answers. Check the call log when you get a gap; anything it escalated will be flagged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A walk-in enquiry" icon="hand-holding-heart">
    Create a **lead**, not a patient. Log what they asked about, build a quotation if they want prices, and set a next-action date before they leave.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Quiet moments — the highest-value 20 minutes in the day

When the floor is calm:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Work the at-risk list" icon="heart-pulse" href="/features/at-risk">
    Top five rows. Draft, read, send.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chase expiring packages" icon="box" href="/features/packages">
    Filter to expiring within 30 days with sessions remaining. Already-paid patients convert fastest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirm tomorrow" icon="calendar-check" href="/features/calendar">
    List view for tomorrow. Confirm every tentative slot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow up overdue leads" icon="clock" href="/features/leads">
    Anything past its next-action date and assigned to you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Closing — last 15 minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the day-end report" icon="chart-line">
    Collections by method against what is in the till and on the card machine. Reconcile now, while the person who took each payment is still here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the inbox" icon="comments">
    Nothing unread, nothing flagged, going into the night.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check tomorrow" icon="calendar-days">
    Any unconfirmed slots left? Send the confirmation request before you leave; overnight replies get handled by Aisha.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note stock" icon="boxes-stacked">
    Anything running low that the automatic deduction would not catch — a broken vial, a sample given away. Record it as wastage with a reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>
