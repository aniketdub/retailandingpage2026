The complete clinical-to-commercial loop for a single visit. Follow it once with a real patient and the product stops feeling like eleven separate screens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check in" icon="door-open">
    Calendar → find the appointment → **Checked in**.

    The clinician's screen now shows this patient as waiting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the consultation" icon="stethoscope">
    From the appointment, click **Start consultation**. RetAI opens your clinic's template for that service.

    Allergies and clinical flags are pinned at the top. They are not in a tab you have to remember to open.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record findings" icon="clipboard-list">
    Fill the structured fields. Where a finding is easier drawn than written, open the [annotation canvas](/features/consultations#the-clinical-drawing-tool), pick the base diagram — face, three-quarter, scalp, or body — and mark it.

    <Tip>
      Number your marks. "Site 1: 0.5ml, site 2: 0.3ml" in the notes, with matching numbered markers on the diagram, is the record you will want at the review visit.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Capture photographs" icon="camera">
    From the consultation, capture *before* images. Tag treatment, area, and angle. Record consent:

    - **Clinical record** — always
    - **Marketing use** — only if the patient has explicitly agreed

    <Warning>
      If marketing consent is not given, do not tick it "to sort out later". A photo without it is structurally barred from any shareable collection, and that is the protection working. Re-consent later if the patient changes their mind.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the prescription" icon="file-lines">
    Start from a template, adjust for the patient, sign, and issue. Deliver as PDF over WhatsApp, or print.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record the plan" icon="box">
    If the plan is a course rather than a single visit, sell it as a **package** — that is what makes session tracking, expiry reminders, and correct revenue recognition work.

    If the patient needs to think about it, create a **quotation** on their lead instead and set a next-action date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deliver the session" icon="syringe">
    Mark today's session delivered. RetAI:

    - increments sessions completed
    - deducts consumables per the procedure's bill of materials
    - recognises the earned portion of revenue and tax
    - queues the next session's reminder
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the appointment" icon="circle-check">
    Mark it **Completed**. This queues the post-visit follow-up and any aftercare message attached to the service.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bill" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Checkout pre-fills from what was delivered. Add anything retail or pharmacy. GST computes per line at each line's own rate.

    Take payment — split across methods if needed — and issue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invoice" icon="whatsapp">
    One click delivers the PDF to the patient's WhatsApp. It is logged on their conversation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book the next visit" icon="calendar-check">
    Before they leave the desk. This is the single highest-leverage action in the entire workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after they walk out

Without anyone touching anything:

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| ~1 hour after completion | Aftercare instructions sent, if configured for that service |
| A few days later | Post-visit follow-up asking how they are getting on |
| Before the next session | Appointment reminder, then a confirmation request if unconfirmed |
| If the gap grows | The patient rises on the [at-risk list](/features/at-risk) with sessions remaining stated |
| As expiry approaches | Package-expiry reminder |
