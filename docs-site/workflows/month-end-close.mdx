About forty minutes on the first working day of the month.

## 1 — Reconcile the money

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm every day-end was run" icon="calendar-days">
    Reports → Day-end, filtered to last month. A missing day means a day nobody reconciled. Find it now, not in the audit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revenue summary" icon="chart-line">
    Collected versus billed for the month. Split by payment method and reconcile against bank and card settlements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Outstanding and ageing" icon="clock">
    Anything past 30 days gets a call this week. Sort by amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retainer and credit balances" icon="wallet">
    Both are liabilities. Rising advance balances mean you are collecting faster than you are delivering — good for cash, but it is treatment you owe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2 — Tax

<Steps>
  <Step title="GST summary" icon="percent">
    Tax collected by rate with taxable value per rate. Export to CSV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check package recognition" icon="box">
    Package revenue should appear as earned, not as collected. Unearned advance is not this month's taxable revenue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review credit notes and refunds" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    Each with its reason and the staff member who authorised it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export for your accountant" icon="download">
    [Data export](/admin/data-export) produces invoices, line items, payments, and the tax breakdown in one archive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3 — Review retention

This is the section that changes what you do next month.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Revenue recovered" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    What at-risk outreach brought back. Compare against your subscription cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Retention rate" icon="repeat">
    Share of patients returning within their expected interval, trended.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Package completion" icon="box">
    Sold sessions actually delivered. Below 70% means your allocation-at-sale discipline is slipping.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No-show analysis" icon="user-xmark">
    Cross-check against reminder delivery. Undelivered reminders are a fixable cause.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## 4 — Review operations

| Report | What you are looking for |
| --- | --- |
| Clinician utilisation | Booked against available hours. Under 60% is a marketing problem; over 90% is a capacity problem. |
| Procedure trends | What is growing, what is fading, period over period |
| Discounts granted | Discount as a share of gross, by staff member |
| Lead conversion | By source and by owner, with lost reasons |
| Consumption by procedure | Two clinicians consuming differently for the same procedure is training or leakage |
| Stock value and wastage | Write-offs and expiry losses |

## 5 — Housekeeping

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stock take" icon="boxes-stacked">
    Physical count against system quantities. Post adjustments with the stock-take reference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Staff and permission review" icon="user-group">
    Anyone left? Deactivate them. Anyone changed role? Update their modules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Price list review" icon="tags">
    Changing prices affects future invoices only. Existing packages hold their agreed price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI transcript sample" icon="robot">
    Read ten AI-handled WhatsApp threads and five voice transcripts. You are checking tone and accuracy, not policing. Anything wrong is usually a service description that needs rewording.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 6 — Set next month's push

Pick one number to move and one action to move it. Examples that work:

- *Package completion below 70%* → allocate every session at the point of sale, without exception
- *No-shows above 12%* → move the reminder from 24h to 24h **and** 2h
- *Utilisation under 60% on Tuesdays* → run a targeted at-risk campaign filtered to Tuesday-preferring patients
