Packages are where most aesthetic revenue lives and where most clinic accounting goes wrong. This walkthrough covers both the clicks and the arithmetic.

## 1 — Quote it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the patient's lead, or create one" icon="hand-holding-heart">
    If they are already a patient, quote from the patient record instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the quotation" icon="pen-to-square">
    Add the package, apply any discount the counsellor is authorised to give. GST computes per line at each line's own rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it" icon="paper-plane">
    Branded PDF over WhatsApp. Set a next-action date on the lead before you close the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2 — Sell it

Convert the accepted quotation straight to checkout — nothing is retyped, so the quoted price and the billed price cannot drift apart.

At checkout, four numbers are tracked separately. Conflating them is the classic error:

| Term | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **List price** | ₹60,000 for 6 laser sessions |
| **Discount** | ₹6,000 granted by the counsellor |
| **Agreed price** | ₹54,000 — what the patient owes |
| **Amount received** | ₹20,000 paid today |

RetAI then derives:

- **Outstanding** — ₹34,000 still to collect
- **Per-session value** — ₹9,000
- **Earned value** — ₹0 today; no session has been delivered
- **Unearned advance** — ₹20,000, a *liability*. You have taken money and owe treatment for it.

<Note>
  This is the point of the module. A six-session package sold today is not six sessions of revenue today, and not six sessions of GST liability today.
</Note>

## 3 — Allocate the sessions

<Frame caption="Six sessions allocated across three months at the configured interval.">
  <img src="/images/workflows/package-01-allocation.png" alt="Package session allocation" />
</Frame>

RetAI proposes dates from the package's configured session interval. Adjust anything, then confirm. Each becomes a real appointment with its own reminder.

<Tip>
  Allocate all sessions at the point of sale, even if dates shift later. A package with sessions on the calendar completes far more often than one the patient is expected to remember to book.
</Tip>

## 4 — Deliver a session

Marking session 1 delivered:

1. Sessions completed → 1, remaining → 5
2. Earned value → ₹9,000; unearned advance → ₹11,000
3. GST recognised on the earned portion, at the correct per-line rate
4. Consumables deducted from stock via the bill of materials
5. Session photograph prompted, bound to session 1
6. Session 2's reminder queued

<Frame caption="Package progress after two of six sessions.">
  <img src="/images/workflows/package-02-progress.png" alt="Package progress tracking" />
</Frame>

### When delivery runs ahead of collection

If you deliver session 3 while only ₹20,000 has been collected, earned value (₹27,000) exceeds amount received. RetAI shows that as **delivered unpaid: ₹7,000** — treatment given away pending collection.

<Warning>
  Delivered-unpaid is the number that quietly grows in clinics without package accounting. Check it on the package report weekly, not at year end.
</Warning>

## 5 — Recover the remainder

RetAI chases sessions without anyone remembering to:

| Trigger | What is sent |
| --- | --- |
| Session interval exceeded | Reminder that the next session is due |
| Package expiry in 30 days with sessions remaining | Expiry reminder |
| Gap widening beyond the patient's own rhythm | Patient rises on the [at-risk list](/features/at-risk) with sessions-remaining stated in the draft |

## 6 — Close it out

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="All sessions delivered" icon="circle-check">
    Package marked complete. Full agreed price recognised. It stays on the patient record as history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Expired with sessions remaining" icon="clock">
    Flagged on the package report. Your policy decides — extend, part-refund, or forfeit. Whatever you choose is recorded with a reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancelled mid-course" icon="circle-xmark">
    Refundable amount computes as amount received minus earned value, adjusted per your policy. Recorded against the original invoice so the audit trail holds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
