## Response targets

| Severity | Definition | Target first response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Service completely unavailable | 1 hour |
| **High** | A core feature broken for your clinic — WhatsApp AI, billing, sign-in | 4 business hours |
| **Normal** | A non-core issue or question | 1 business day |
| **Low** | Feature request, general question | 3 business days |

Business hours are IST. Full terms: [Service levels](/security/sla).

## What to include

<Steps>
  <Step title="What you were doing" icon="hand-pointer">
    The screen and the action. "Taking payment on the checkout screen."
  </Step>
  <Step title="What you expected" icon="lightbulb">
    "The invoice to show 18% GST on the serum."
  </Step>
  <Step title="What happened" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    "It showed 5%."
  </Step>
  <Step title="A reference" icon="hashtag">
    Invoice number, patient phone, appointment time. This is what turns a two-day exchange into a one-message fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When" icon="clock">
    Roughly is fine. It lets us find the request in the logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A screenshot" icon="camera">
    Almost always worth it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not send patient names, photographs, or clinical details in a support message. Send the invoice number or the patient's internal reference instead — we can look up what we need from our side without you transmitting PHI over a support channel.
</Warning>

## Support access to your data

RetentionOS staff hold **no standing access** to your clinic's data after handover. Where a problem genuinely requires it, we ask in your WhatsApp group, access is time-boxed, and it is logged.

<Tip>
  Ask to see that log any time. If a vendor cannot tell you exactly when their staff accessed your patient data, that answers a more important question than the one you asked.
</Tip>

## Feature requests

Send them to the WhatsApp group or support@retentionos.tech. Tell us the **problem**, not only the feature — "the front desk forgets to rebook before the patient leaves" gets you a better answer than "add a rebook button".

Shipped changes appear in the [changelog](/help/changelog).
