Clinic software fails at rollout, not at build. RetAI onboarding is a guided programme run by our team with yours — you are never handed a login and left to work it out.

**Typical duration: 7–10 working days** from kickoff to going live, depending on how quickly your existing patient data reaches us.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shared WhatsApp group" icon="whatsapp">
    We open a WhatsApp group with your clinic owner, front-desk lead, and the RetentionOS onboarding engineer. Every question during rollout goes here — no ticket queues, no waiting for email.

    This group stays open permanently. It becomes your support channel after go-live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clinic data handover" icon="folder-open">
    We collect, in one pass:

    - Clinic logo, letterhead, registration and GSTIN details
    - Staff list with roles and the modules each should see
    - Service and procedure catalogue with prices, GST rates, and durations
    - Package definitions — sessions, validity, price
    - Existing patient records and visit history
    - Consumables and stock, if you want inventory tracked from day one

    <Tip>
      Whatever format you already have works — an Excel sheet, a CSV export from your current software, even photographs of a printed price list. We normalise it. See [Data import](/admin/data-import) for the fields that matter.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Backend setup and customisation" icon="gear">
    Our engineer configures your tenant:

    - Clinic record, branding, and invoice template
    - Service catalogue, GST rates, package structures
    - Consultation and prescription templates for your specialty
    - Role permissions matrix
    - Follow-up and reminder timing rules
    - The AI agent's persona, tone, and the claims it is forbidden from making

    <Note>
      Your data sits in its own tenant from the first row written. See [Architecture](/security/architecture) for how clinic isolation is enforced.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Live training for your team" icon="chalkboard-user">
    Two online sessions, recorded so new joiners can watch them later:

    | Session | Audience | Covers |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Front desk | Reception, coordinators | Calendar, patients, WhatsApp inbox, packages, billing |
    | Clinical & owner | Doctors, owner | Consultations, Rx, photo library, at-risk, reports |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Practice on a demo account" icon="flask">
    Your team gets a sandbox tenant with fake patients. Book, bill, prescribe, refund, break things — none of it touches real records.

    We do not move to the next step until your front desk can complete a full check-in → consultation → invoice → follow-up loop unaided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handover to your super admin" icon="user-shield">
    Ownership of the tenant transfers to your nominated super admin. From this point RetentionOS staff hold no standing access to your clinic's data — support access is time-boxed and requested in the WhatsApp group.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Password reset across all accounts" icon="key">
    Every account created during setup resets its password. No credential that passed through onboarding remains valid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="WhatsApp Business integration" icon="comments">
    We connect your WhatsApp Business number and verify:

    - Inbound patient messages arrive in the inbox and attach to the right patient
    - Aisha replies correctly and books into real free slots
    - Reminders, follow-ups, and invoice delivery all send
    - The number is verified with the correct display name

    Full detail: [Connect WhatsApp](/admin/integrations/whatsapp).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live" icon="rocket">
    Your clinic starts running on RetAI: scheduling, patient records, billing, and patient communication. Our engineer is in the WhatsApp group throughout the first day.

    <Warning>
      Keep your previous system readable — not writable — for 30 days. Nobody should be entering data in two places, but you want the old records available while your team builds muscle memory.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Continuous support" icon="life-ring">
    The WhatsApp group stays open. Response commitments are in the [SLA](/security/sla). Product updates land in the [changelog](/help/changelog).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What we need from you

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One decision-maker" icon="user-tie">
    A single person who can approve the service list, prices, and permissions. Rollouts stall on committees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your patient data, once" icon="database">
    In any format. The sooner it arrives, the sooner at-risk scoring has history to work with.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A WhatsApp Business number" icon="whatsapp">
    Either a new number or your existing clinic number, provided it is not already bound to the WhatsApp consumer app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Two hours of your team's time" icon="clock">
    Split across the two training sessions. Practice time is self-paced.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
