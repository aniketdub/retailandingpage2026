This is the fastest path from an empty RetAI account to a working clinic. It assumes your account has already been created for you — see the [onboarding plan](/onboarding) if it has not.

<Note>
  You need **Owner** access for steps 2–5. A receptionist or doctor account can complete steps 1, 6, and 7 only.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in" icon="key">
    Open [www.retentionos.tech](https://www.retentionos.tech) and sign in with your work email.

    Two methods work:

    - **Google** — one tap, recommended. Your Google account must already be registered as a staff member; RetAI never lets an unrecognised Google account create itself an account.
    - **Email and password** — issued to you during onboarding.

    <Frame caption="The RetAI sign-in screen.">
      <img src="/images/quickstart/01-login.png" alt="RetAI login screen" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      If sign-in says your account is not recognised, your staff record has not been created yet. Contact your clinic owner — this is a permissions issue, not a password issue.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your team" icon="user-group">
    Go to **CRM → Staff**. Add every person who will use RetAI, with the right role:

    | Role | Sees by default |
    | --- | --- |
    | Owner | Everything. Cannot be restricted. |
    | Doctor | Everything except Staff management |
    | Receptionist | Calendar, patients, leads, packages, gallery, WhatsApp — not billing, inventory, or prescriptions |

    You can override any of these per clinic. See [Roles & permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).

    <Frame caption="Staff management with per-role module toggles.">
      <img src="/images/quickstart/02-staff.png" alt="Staff and role permissions" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Load your services and prices" icon="tags">
    Go to **CRM → Services**. Each service needs a name, a price, a GST rate, and a duration.

    Duration matters more than it looks: it is what the AI uses to find a real free slot when a patient asks for an appointment. A service with the wrong duration produces double-booked chairs.

    <Tip>
      Bulk-loading a long price list? Send it to us as a spreadsheet during onboarding and we import it for you — see [Data import](/admin/data-import).
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import your patients" icon="users">
    Patients can arrive three ways:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Bulk import (recommended for go-live)" icon="file-csv">
        Send us a CSV with name, phone, email, last visit date, and treatment history. We normalise the phone numbers to E.164 and match duplicates before anything is written. Details in [Data import](/admin/data-import).
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Created by the AI" icon="robot">
        The first time an unknown number messages your WhatsApp line, RetAI creates a patient record for it automatically.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Added by hand" icon="user-plus">
        **Patients → Add patient**. Fine for walk-ins.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    <Warning>
      Phone numbers are the join key between WhatsApp, your patient list, and the message log. A number stored as `98765 43210` instead of `+919876543210` will not match an inbound WhatsApp message, and the patient will silently appear as a stranger. The importer fixes this; manual entry does not always.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp">
    This is the step that turns RetAI from a database into a retention engine. Follow [Connect WhatsApp](/admin/integrations/whatsapp).

    Once connected:

    - Inbound patient messages land in the [WhatsApp Inbox](/features/chats)
    - Aisha replies, answers questions about your treatments, and books appointments
    - Appointment reminders and follow-ups send themselves
    - Invoices can be delivered to the patient as a PDF link
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book one real appointment" icon="calendar-days">
    Open the [AI Calendar](/features/calendar) and create an appointment for a real patient tomorrow.

    Watch what happens automatically:

    1. A reminder is queued for the right time before the slot
    2. If Google Calendar is connected, the event appears on the clinician's calendar
    3. The patient's record shows the upcoming visit

    <Frame caption="The calendar, with resource columns per clinician.">
      <img src="/images/quickstart/03-calendar.png" alt="AI Calendar day view" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your first AI message" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Open **At-Risk Patients**, pick the patient at the top of the list, and click **Draft message**.

    RetAI writes a message grounded in that patient's actual history — the treatment they had, how long it has been, what they were part-way through. Read it, edit it if you want, and send.

    <Frame caption="An AI-drafted win-back message, before sending.">
      <img src="/images/quickstart/04-ai-draft.png" alt="AI drafted reminder" />
    </Frame>

    That is the whole loop: RetAI notices the patient drifting, writes the message, sends it, handles the reply, and books the slot.
  </Step>
</Steps>
