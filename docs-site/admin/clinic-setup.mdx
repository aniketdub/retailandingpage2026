## Clinic profile

| Field | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic name | Invoices, prescriptions, patient messages |
| Logo | Invoice letterhead, prescription header, photo watermark |
| Address and contact | Invoice footer, what Aisha tells patients who ask for directions |
| GSTIN | Tax invoices |
| Registration numbers | Prescriptions and statutory documents |
| Time zone | Every timestamp in the product |

## Invoice settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Numbering" icon="hashtag" defaultOpen>
    Prefix, starting number, and reset behaviour — never, annually, or monthly. Numbers are allocated atomically from a per-clinic sequence: two tills billing at the same instant cannot collide.

    <Warning>
      Changing the prefix or resetting the sequence mid-year will confuse your accountant and possibly your tax filing. Change it at a financial-year boundary or not at all.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Terms and footer" icon="file-lines">
    Payment terms, cancellation policy, and any statutory text your invoices must carry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Default GST behaviour" icon="percent">
    Whether catalogue prices are entered GST-inclusive or exclusive. Set once — changing it later restates how every existing price is interpreted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Working hours

Clinic opening hours per day, plus holidays and closures. This bounds the calendar and bounds what the AI can offer — Aisha will not book a slot on a day the clinic is shut.

Per-clinician hours are separate and live in [Staff](/features/staff).

## Automation timing

The rules that decide when RetAI messages a patient.

| Automation | Typical setting |
| --- | --- |
| Appointment reminder | 24 hours before, and 2 hours before |
| Confirmation request | On tentative bookings, 48 hours before |
| Post-visit follow-up | 3 days after a completed appointment |
| Aftercare | 1 hour after completion, for services configured to send it |
| Package session due | At the configured session interval |
| Package expiry warning | 30 days before expiry, if sessions remain |
| At-risk re-attempt | 7 days after an unanswered win-back |
| Quiet hours | No automated message before 09:00 or after 20:00 |

<Tip>
  Quiet hours are worth respecting even when a reminder is technically due. A clinic that messages at 11pm gets blocked by patients, and a blocked number cannot reach anyone.
</Tip>

## AI agent settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Persona and tone" icon="robot">
    The agent's name, greeting, and register — formal, warm, or brisk. Set to match how your front desk actually speaks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Booking permission" icon="calendar-check">
    Whether the agent may create appointments or only capture the request for a human. Start restrictive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Escalation rules" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Which topics always go to a human. Clinical questions and complaints are non-removable; you can add more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Forbidden claims" icon="ban">
    Phrases and claims the agent must never make. Outcome promises and medical claims are enforced by default.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
