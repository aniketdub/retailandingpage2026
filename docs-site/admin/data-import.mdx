Migration is run by the RetentionOS team during [onboarding](/onboarding). This page tells you what to send and what happens to it.

<Note>
  Send whatever format you already have — a CSV export, an Excel workbook, even photographs of a printed price list. We normalise it. The fields below are what we extract, not a format you must produce.
</Note>

## Patients

**Required**

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | |
| Phone | Any format. We normalise to E.164 (`+919876543210`). |

**Strongly recommended**

| Field | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Last visit date | Without it, at-risk scoring has no baseline and stays quiet for weeks |
| Treatment history | Drives at-risk cadence and gives Aisha context in conversation |
| Package/session state | Unredeemed sessions are your fastest recoverable revenue |
| Email, DOB, gender, address | Reporting and demographics |
| Referral source | Lead-source reporting |
| Allergies and clinical flags | Safety. Send these even if you send nothing else optional. |
| Outstanding balance | So the front desk sees it on day one |

<Warning>
  **Phone numbers are the identity key.** A number stored without a country code will not match an inbound WhatsApp message, and that patient will appear to RetAI as a stranger. Our importer fixes what it can and reports what it cannot — review that report.
</Warning>

## Services

Name, category, price, GST rate, duration, and the clinicians who deliver it. Add the patient-facing description if you have one; if not, we draft them and you approve — this is what Aisha reads to patients.

## Packages

Name, component services with session counts, list price, validity period, and session interval.

**In-flight packages** — courses your patients have already partly used — need sessions total, sessions already delivered, agreed price, and amount received. Without those four numbers, revenue recognition starts wrong and stays wrong.

## Inventory

Product name, SKU, unit, current quantity, purchase price, selling price, GST rate, reorder level, and batch/expiry where applicable. Vendors optional at go-live.

## Staff

Name, work email, phone, role, and — for clinicians — registration number, specialty, and which services they deliver.

## What happens to your file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Validation" icon="magnifying-glass">
    We check for missing required fields, malformed phone numbers, impossible dates, and duplicate records. You get a report before anything is written.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Normalisation" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Phone numbers to E.164, dates to a single format, currency strings to numbers, names to consistent casing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Duplicate matching" icon="clone">
    Candidate duplicates are matched on phone first, then name plus date of birth. Ambiguous cases go on a review list rather than being merged silently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dry run" icon="flask">
    Imported into your demo tenant first. Your team checks a sample against the source before anything reaches the live tenant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live import" icon="database">
    Written to your live tenant, with an import reference on every record so the batch is reversible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After import

- Check ten patients at random against your old system
- Confirm package session counts on your five most valuable in-flight packages
- Confirm at-risk begins populating — it needs last-visit dates to have imported
- Test one WhatsApp message from an imported patient's number and confirm it attaches to the right record

<Tip>
  The WhatsApp test is the one people skip and the one that catches phone-normalisation problems. Do it before go-live, not after.
</Tip>

## Ongoing import

Beyond migration, you can bulk-add via CSV in **Patients → Import** and **Services → Import**, or push records from a website form through the [API](/api-reference/endpoints/crm).
