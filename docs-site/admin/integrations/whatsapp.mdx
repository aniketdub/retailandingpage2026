This is the integration that turns RetAI from a records system into a retention engine. It is set up by the RetentionOS team during [onboarding](/onboarding); this page explains what happens and what you need to have ready.

## What you need

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A phone number" icon="phone">
    Either a new number or your existing clinic number — provided it is **not currently registered on the WhatsApp consumer or Business app**. A number can only live in one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business verification details" icon="building">
    Registered clinic name, address, website, and business category. These are what appear as your verified display name.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  If your clinic number is currently in use on the WhatsApp Business *app* on someone's phone, migrating it to the API means that app stops working for that number. Decide this before you start — reversing it is slow. Most clinics take a fresh number and forward the old one.
</Warning>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="We provision the number" icon="phone">
    Registered against your clinic's tenant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business verification" icon="circle-check">
    Submitted with your details. Verification typically takes 1–3 business days and is what gets your clinic name shown to patients instead of a bare number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Webhook connection" icon="plug">
    Inbound messages are routed to your clinic. Every inbound request is signature-verified — a forged webhook is rejected before it reaches any data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message templates" icon="file-lines">
    Reminders, confirmations, and follow-ups are submitted for approval. Approval usually takes hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent configuration" icon="robot">
    Aisha's persona, tone, escalation rules, and forbidden claims are set from your [clinic settings](/admin/clinic-setup#ai-agent-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="End-to-end test" icon="flask">
    We verify, with you watching:

    - A message from a known patient's number attaches to the right record
    - A message from an unknown number creates a patient
    - Aisha answers a treatment question correctly from your catalogue
    - Aisha books an appointment into a genuinely free slot
    - A reminder sends at the configured time
    - An invoice delivers as a PDF link
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Templates versus conversation

WhatsApp distinguishes two message types, and the distinction shapes what RetAI can do when.

| Type | When usable | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Template** | Any time, pre-approved wording | Reminders, confirmations, follow-ups, win-backs |
| **Session** | Within 24 hours of the patient's last message | Free-form conversation, including everything Aisha says |

<Tip>
  This is why a patient reply matters so much. Once they message you, a 24-hour window opens in which the conversation can be completely natural. Aisha is designed to get useful work done inside that window rather than pushing more templates at them.
</Tip>

## Reliability properties worth knowing

- **Inbound messages are deduplicated at the database level.** WhatsApp retries webhooks on any hiccup; a retry cannot produce a second reply from Aisha.
- **Outbound messages cannot double-send.** Queued messages are claimed atomically before delivery.
- **Inbound is rate-limited per phone number.** A malfunctioning or malicious sender cannot exhaust your AI quota.
- **Unattributable messages are dropped, not guessed.** If a message cannot be resolved to a clinic with certainty, nothing is written and no reply is sent. Silence beats replying into the wrong clinic's context.

## Number reputation

WhatsApp scores business numbers on how patients react to them. A number that gets blocked or reported drops in quality and eventually loses sending capacity.

RetAI protects yours by:

- Rate-limiting and spreading bulk sends over time
- Respecting quiet hours on automated messages
- Not re-sending an unanswered message more than the configured attempts
- Honouring opt-outs permanently

<Warning>
  The fastest way to damage a number is a large blast of near-identical promotional messages. Use the [at-risk list](/features/at-risk), which personalises per patient, rather than treating RetAI as a broadcast tool.
</Warning>

## Opt-outs

A patient who asks to stop receiving messages is opted out permanently and immediately. They can still be replied to if *they* message you; they will receive no automated message again unless they explicitly opt back in.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Messages arrive but attach to no patient" icon="user-xmark">
    A phone-format mismatch. The patient record likely holds a number without a country code. Fix the record; history reattaches.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Aisha is not replying" icon="robot">
    Check that the thread has not been taken over by a staff member — a taken-over thread is deliberately silent from the AI. Then check the agent is enabled in clinic settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reminders are not sending" icon="clock">
    Check template approval status, and check the reminder is not falling inside quiet hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong display name" icon="id-card">
    Business verification is incomplete or the display name is pending review. This is on WhatsApp's side; we chase it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

More in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
