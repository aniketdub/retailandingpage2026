Push notifications are how a clinic owner finds out a patient messaged at 9pm without watching the inbox.

## Enabling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the mobile app" icon="mobile-screen-button">
    iOS or Android. See [Mobile app](/features/mobile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in" icon="key">
    Same account as the web.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications when prompted" icon="bell">
    If you dismissed the prompt, enable it in your device's system settings for the RetAI app. The app cannot re-prompt once dismissed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which alerts you want" icon="sliders">
    Settings → Notifications, per device. Two phones can have different settings for the same account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available notifications

| Notification | Default | Recommended for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound patient WhatsApp message | On | Front desk, owner |
| Thread escalated by the AI | On | Whoever handles escalations |
| Appointment booked by the AI | Off | Owner, during the first fortnight |
| Appointment cancelled by a patient | On | Front desk |
| Overdue lead follow-up assigned to you | On | Anyone who owns leads |
| Low stock alert | Off | Owner, practice manager |
| Day-end report ready | Off | Owner |

## Content-free by design

<Note>
  Push payloads never contain patient names or message content. The notification tells you *that* a message arrived; tapping it opens the app, which then loads the data under your authenticated session.
</Note>

That means a notification sitting on a lock screen in a waiting room discloses nothing. It also means you cannot read a message from the notification shade — you have to open the app. That trade is deliberate.

## Device management

Each device that signs in registers itself for push. Signing out deregisters it.

<Warning>
  Sold or lost phone? Sign out on it if you can. If you cannot, have an owner deactivate and reactivate your staff account — that invalidates the session and stops delivery to that device.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No notifications at all" icon="bell-slash">
    Check system notification permission for the RetAI app, then check per-notification toggles in Settings → Notifications, then sign out and back in to re-register the device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Delayed notifications" icon="clock">
    Usually battery optimisation on Android suspending background delivery. Exclude RetAI from battery optimisation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications on the wrong device" icon="mobile-screen">
    An old device still holds a registration. Sign out on it, or have an owner cycle your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duplicate notifications" icon="clone">
    The same account signed in on two devices with the same alerts enabled. Turn the alert off on one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
