## Encryption

| Data | Protection |
| --- | --- |
| In transit | TLS on every connection — browser, mobile app, API, and every vendor interface |
| At rest | Encrypted at the storage layer, including database, images, and call recordings |
| Google refresh tokens | Additionally encrypted with **AES-256-GCM**, under a key held separately from the application session secret |
| Backups | Encrypted |

<Note>
  The Google token key is deliberately separate from the session secret. Rotating one does not invalidate the other — a security operation on sessions should not silently break every clinic's calendar sync.
</Note>

## Clinical images

Before-and-after photographs are the most sensitive category RetAI holds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consent is structural" icon="file-signature">
    Clinical-record consent and marketing consent are recorded separately. A photo without marketing consent is **incapable** of entering a shareable collection — the export path refuses it, rather than relying on someone remembering.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signed, expiring URLs" icon="link-slash">
    Images are served only through short-lived signed URLs to authenticated staff. A URL copied out of the browser expires and cannot be forwarded as a permanent link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Identity masking" icon="eye-slash">
    Where marketing consent covers the treatment area but not facial identity, masking is required before an export completes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watermarking" icon="droplet">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Call recordings

Recorded only where voice is enabled. A configurable consent announcement plays at call start.

<Warning>
  The announcement wording is the clinic's legal responsibility. It must match what your jurisdiction requires for recording a call. Have it reviewed before go-live.
</Warning>

Recordings are encrypted at rest, scoped to your clinic, and deleted automatically at your configured retention period.

## Push notifications

Payloads never contain patient names or message content. A notification says a message arrived; the app loads the content afterwards under the recipient's authenticated session. A notification visible on a lock screen discloses nothing.

## Message content and AI processing

Patient message content is sent to Google Gemini for reply generation. Understand what that means:

- Only an **allowlisted subset** of patient fields accompanies the message — not the full record
- The clinic and patient are already resolved before the model is called
- Every AI decision is logged with the context it was given, so what the AI was told is auditable
- Processing occurs in the United States. Disclosed in [Subprocessors](/security/subprocessors).

## Cross-border processing

Several subprocessors operate outside India. This is disclosed rather than buried:

| Vendor | Role | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vercel | Application hosting | United States, global edge |
| Supabase | Database and storage | Region selected at project setup |
| Twilio | WhatsApp delivery | United States |
| Meta | WhatsApp network | United States |
| Google | AI processing, Calendar, Sign-In | United States |
| Bolna | Voice | Confirm current region |
| Firebase | Push (device token only) | United States |

The DPDP Act permits cross-border transfer except to countries restricted by government notification. Full detail: [Subprocessors](/security/subprocessors).

## Patient rights

Under India's DPDP Act, patients may access, correct, and request erasure of their data. RetAI provides the mechanisms; the **clinic is the Data Fiduciary** and decides each request.

| Right | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Patient → Actions → Download summary, or Export full record |
| Correction | Edit the patient record; changes are audited |
| Erasure | Delete the patient record, subject to medical-record retention law |
| Withdraw marketing consent | Toggle on the patient record; photos immediately become unusable for marketing |
| Stop messages | Opt-out, honoured permanently and immediately |

<Warning>
  Erasure is not unconditional. Medical-record retention obligations under Indian law may require you to keep clinical records for a minimum period regardless of a patient's request. That assessment is the clinic's to make, with its own legal advice — RetAI does not make it for you.
</Warning>

## Staff device security

RetAI stores no local copy of patient data on staff devices. The mobile app is a thin authenticated shell around the same secure application the web serves.

A lost phone is a session to revoke, not a database to recover. Deactivating and reactivating the staff account invalidates it.
