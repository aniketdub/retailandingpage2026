## Current subprocessors

| Subprocessor | Role | Data processed | Location | Cross-border |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | :---: |
| **Vercel Inc.** | Application hosting | All application traffic. No direct database access. | United States, global edge | Yes |
| **Supabase Inc.** | Database and storage | All customer data — patients, appointments, invoices, messages, images, staff | Region selected at project setup | If region is outside India |
| **Twilio Inc.** | WhatsApp message delivery | Patient phone numbers; message content | United States | Yes |
| **Meta Platforms, Inc.** | WhatsApp Business Platform | Patient phone numbers; message content, as network operator | United States | Yes |
| **Google LLC** | AI processing (Gemini); Google Calendar; Google Sign-In | Message content sent for reply generation; calendar event data where connected; email and name for sign-in | United States | Yes |
| **Bolna** | AI voice calling | Patient phone numbers; call audio and transcripts, where voice is enabled | Confirm current region | Confirm |
| **Firebase (Google LLC)** | Push notifications | **Device push token only.** Notification content is generic — never a patient name or message text. | United States | Token only; no patient data |

## Notes on scope

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vercel does not hold your data" icon="server">
    It serves the application. Patient records live in the database, which Vercel does not access directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google receives message content, not whole records" icon="robot">
    Only an allowlisted subset of patient fields accompanies a message to the AI. Not the full clinical record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Firebase receives no patient data at all" icon="bell">
    Push payloads carry a device token and a generic string. This is why a notification on a lock screen discloses nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bolna only where voice is enabled" icon="phone">
    If your plan does not include the voice receptionist, no data reaches Bolna.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cross-border transfer

Where a subprocessor processes data outside India, this is disclosed under our Privacy Policy and DPA. The DPDP Act permits cross-border transfer except to countries restricted by government notification.

## HIPAA

<Warning>
  RetentionOS does **not** currently hold Business Associate Agreements with these vendors, and RetAI is not offered as a HIPAA-compliant product. It is built for the Indian market and the DPDP Act. If you require HIPAA coverage, we are not currently the right vendor — and we would rather say so here than in a procurement questionnaire.
</Warning>
