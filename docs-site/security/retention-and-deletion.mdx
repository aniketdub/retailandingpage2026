## While your subscription is active

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Patient records, appointments, treatment plans, invoices | For the duration of your subscription, per your configuration and applicable healthcare record-keeping law |
| WhatsApp message content and call transcripts | For the duration of the subscription, unless you delete a conversation |
| Clinical images | For the duration of the subscription, or your configured image retention period if shorter |
| Call recordings | Your configured retention period, then automatically deleted |
| Staff accounts and access logs | Subscription duration plus 12 months, for security investigation |
| Audit log | Minimum 3 years, **even where the underlying record has been deleted** |

<Note>
  The audit log outliving the records it describes is deliberate. Deleting a patient does not delete the record that a patient was deleted, when, and by whom. Without that, deletion itself becomes untraceable.
</Note>

## When you delete something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Removed from primary storage" icon="trash">
    Immediately. It stops appearing anywhere in the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Persists in encrypted backups" icon="database">
    For up to 30 days, until backups rotate. This is standard practice for maintaining recoverability, and it is a real property you should know about rather than one we obscure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purged as backups rotate" icon="circle-check">
    No further copy remains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit entry remains" icon="clipboard-list">
    The deletion event itself is retained as an integrity record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deletion is not instantaneous everywhere. If you are actioning an erasure request under a legal deadline, the primary-storage deletion is immediate; complete purge from backups follows the rotation schedule above. Say this accurately to a patient rather than promising instant total erasure.
</Warning>

## Patient erasure requests

Your clinic is the Data Fiduciary and evaluates each request, including any exception — medical-record retention obligations under other Indian law may require you to keep clinical records regardless.

RetAI actions a deletion instruction from you within **15 business days**.

## If you leave RetAI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export window — 30 days from termination" icon="download">
    Your complete dataset stays available for export. Full clinic archive plus all clinical images. See [Data export](/admin/data-export).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Primary deletion — within a further 30 days" icon="trash">
    Deleted from primary storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Backup purge — maximum 90 days from termination" icon="database">
    Aged out of encrypted backups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Minimal record retained" icon="file-lines">
    Clinic name, subscription dates, and billing history, as required for our own tax and accounting obligations under Indian law.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Export before you need to, not after. The 30-day window is generous but it is a window, and the last week of a vendor relationship is not when you want to discover you needed something.
</Tip>

## Backups

Encrypted, rolling, retained for disaster recovery. They are not a per-record undo — a record deleted from primary storage ages out of backups rather than being surgically removed from every copy.

<Warning>
  If you delete something by accident, tell us **immediately**. Recovery from backup is possible within the rotation window but it restores to a point in time, which has consequences for everything else changed since. It is a last resort, not a routine operation.
</Warning>

## Configurable retention

| Setting | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Call recording retention | [Voice setup](/admin/integrations/voice) |
| Clinical image retention | [Clinic setup](/admin/clinic-setup#photo-settings) |
| Conversation deletion | Per conversation, in the [WhatsApp Inbox](/features/chats) |

<Warning>
  Do not set retention shorter than your medical-record obligations. A clinic that auto-deletes clinical images at 12 months may be deleting records it is legally required to hold. Confirm the period with your own legal advice before shortening it.
</Warning>
